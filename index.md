# About

I'm Atikul Gazi, a **Staff Engineer I at Samsung R&D Institute Bangladesh**, based in Dhaka. Since joining Samsung in May 2016, I have worked on the SmartThings iOS app, connected-device features, camera streaming and native SDK integration. I enjoy the engineering behind reliable user experiences: clear APIs, predictable asynchronous behaviour and software that works beyond the happy path.

Most of my career has focused on the SmartThings app. More recently, my work has moved into **SCClient**, bridging native Swift iOS APIs with a C/C++ connectivity layer. Along the way, I have led a four-engineer camera-plugin team and collaborated with colleagues across multiple countries.

I use AI-assisted development tools in my engineering workflow and explore **on-device AI on iOS**. My recent proof-of-concept work includes authoring and adapting lightweight Hugging Face models for integration into Xcode projects. This is hands-on exploration, not a claim of production AI deployment.

## Selected work

### SmartThings iOS — connected experiences

Contributed to device connectivity, smart-home features and native APIs used by plugins. Built more than 100 native APIs connecting plugin functionality with iOS capabilities. My work spans feature delivery, SDK integration, diagnostics and resolving issues reported by users.

[SmartThings on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/smartthings/id1222822904)

### Home Care Monitor — camera streaming

Led a four-engineer camera-plugin team, working on live streaming, playback and camera controls. This work brought together iOS development, streaming integration and the practical challenges of connected devices and asynchronous events.

### SCClient — Swift and C/C++ interoperability

Contribute to the iOS SDK layer that connects native APIs with C/C++ connectivity services. Areas of work include subscriptions and caching, asynchronous callbacks, object lifetime and cleanup, connection recovery, diagnostics and framework delivery.

### On-device AI — model authoring and integration

Explore lightweight models from Hugging Face, model authoring and optimization workflows, and integration into iOS projects through Xcode. My public repository is a fork used for learning and experimentation; it is not an original AI framework.

[Explore my model-learning repository](https://github.com/atiksplanet/coreai-models)
